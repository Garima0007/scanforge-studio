## Featured Project

### QRForge Pro | Advanced QR and Barcode Studio

QRForge Pro is a browser-based QR and barcode platform with an optional Node.js backend. It combines a customizable QR designer, 16+ content types, live camera scanning, image decoding, bulk generation, print sheets, analytics, high-resolution exports, and encrypted QR payloads.

**Built with:** HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, Web Crypto API, Node.js, Express, and SQLite.

**Highlights:**

- Custom QR shapes, colors, gradients, logos, themes, and call-to-action frames
- Website, Wi-Fi, vCard, payment, event, social, location, document, and barcode formats
- AES-GCM encryption with PBKDF2 for password-protected QR content
- Live camera scanning, image decoding, scan history, and export tools
- Bulk generation, ZIP export, print-sheet creation, and scan analytics
- Progressive Web App support with browser-only and server-backed modes
